A sales channel, not merely a catalogue

01 — A sales channel, not merely a catalogue

The challenge was to move a complex furniture range onto a phone without losing the logic of selection. Customers need filters, cart and clear ordering; managers need lead control and behavioural insight.

02 — One product from UX to release

We designed the mobile journeys, interface and technical architecture. The native SwiftUI app uses Firebase, Monobank acquiring, behavioural analytics and a dedicated manager dashboard.

03 — A real product in production

The product travelled from first mockup to App Store publication. Source code, operating flows and access credentials remain part of a manageable product system.
